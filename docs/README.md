# Energy Lab field guide

Energy Lab includes steam, wind, and solar PV operating-point simulators. The interactive field guide here focuses on the steam Rankine cycle; the other sources use separate resource and conversion models.

## Start here

1. Open the [Energy Lab simulator](../index.html) or the deployed [Energy Lab](https://turbinelab.aadityahasabnis.com/).
2. Choose steam, wind, or solar PV from the energy-source selector.
3. Change one input at a time and inspect the output, curve, and energy boundary.
4. Use **Compare** to pin the renewable setup before changing controls; steam has its own comparison control.
5. Read the [model notes](energy-models.md) for assumptions and limitations.

## Learning objective

Explain how each source converts a resource into electricity, and identify which assumptions control the estimate. The wind and PV controls show a single operating point; they do not predict annual output or site performance.
