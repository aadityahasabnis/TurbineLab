EIGHT QUICK FACTS · ONE POWERFUL LOOP

Steam is more interesting than it looks.

Little changes in pressure and phase can reshape an entire power cycle. Here are eight facts to spark a better experiment.

THE CORE IDEA
HEAT→WORK→POWER

Energy changes form. It does not disappear.

01 PRESSURE
↑

Pressure moves the boiling point.

Water boils at a higher temperature when its pressure rises. Steam temperature only tells the full story when you know the pressure too.

P AND T BELONG TOGETHER
02 PHASE CHANGE
◌

Boiling can absorb a lot of heat.

During a phase change, energy can go into turning liquid into vapor while temperature stays near the saturation temperature for that pressure.

ENERGY IN · TEMPERATURE NEARLY STEADY
03 THE PUMP
↗

Pumping liquid is the clever part.

At 100 °C and atmospheric pressure, saturated steam occupies roughly 1,600 times the volume of the same mass of liquid water. The pump handles dense liquid, not that huge vapor volume.

LIQUID IS MUCH DENSER
04 QUALITY
x

“100% quality” is not “extra dry.”

Steam quality is the vapor mass fraction inside a liquid–vapor mixture. Superheated vapor lies outside that mixture region, so its quality is undefined.

QUALITY ONLY INSIDE THE DOME
05 CRITICAL POINT
◇

Liquid and vapor can stop being distinct.

Water’s critical point is about 22.064 MPa and 373.946 °C. Above it there is no ordinary boiling boundary between liquid and vapor.

A PROPERTY-MODEL BOUNDARY
06 POWER
⚡

A small unit trick saves big mistakes.

One kilogram per second carrying 1 kJ/kg of energy change transfers 1 kW. Divide kW by 1,000 to display MW.

kg/s × kJ/kg = kJ/s = kW
07 EFFICIENCY
%

More flow can mean more power, not more efficiency.

At the same steam states, heat and work rates both scale with mass flow. Their ratio can stay nearly constant while total power rises.

RATES SCALE · RATIOS MAY NOT
08 ACCOUNTING
↺

A Sankey diagram needs a boundary.

Change what counts as useful: condenser heat may be “waste” for electricity-only generation but a useful output in combined heat and power.
